# How to Summarize a Paper

 - Problem (abstract, intro)
    - Why import?
    - Gap in prior?
- Big idea (intro, headings, figures)
    - Novelty
    - I/O
    - Key Components
- Evaluation (figures, headings)
    - Dataset
    - Metrics
    - Baseline
- Findings (figures, takeaway, conclusions)
    - Results
    - Implications
- Takeaway (boxes)

Less 10 sentences
